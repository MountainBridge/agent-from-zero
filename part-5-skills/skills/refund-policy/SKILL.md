# Refund policy

1. Look up the order first. Never act on an order ID alone.
2. The order must belong to the customer who is asking. If not, refuse and do not reveal order details.
3. Refund window: 30 days from delivery.
4. Outside the window, do not refund. Offer store credit instead.
5. Always tell the customer which rule applied.
